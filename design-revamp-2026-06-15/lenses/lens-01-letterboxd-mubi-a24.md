# Lens 01 — Taste-class Dark Cinema (Letterboxd · MUBI · A24 · Criterion)

## VERDICT (3 sentences max)
BollyAI already has the dark-cinema *materials* (OKLCH near-black, Fraunces, JetBrains numbers) but spends them like a content farm, not a tastemaker: the homepage is a flat egalitarian wall where nothing is sacred and nothing is scarce. The taste-tier's real moat is not the dark palette, it is **editorial scarcity + a personal diary loop** - MUBI makes *one* thing matter per day, Letterboxd makes *your own logged history* the reason you come back. BollyAI must steal both with zero login: a single commanding **"Aaj ka Faisla" (Today's Verdict) stage** above the fold, and a **localStorage diary** ("Maine dekhi / Meri Watchlist") that turns the visitor's own taste into the retention hook the brand currently has no answer for.

## WHAT THE BEST DO  (named examples + the MECHANISM)
- **One scarce hero, not a wall** — *MUBI* ("one handpicked film, 30 days, then it's gone") and *Criterion Channel*'s single rotating spotlight. Mechanism: scarcity defeats the *paradox of choice* (cited verbatim in MUBI brand research) — by curating to ONE, they manufacture authority and an act of trust. A bento of 9 equal tiles says "we have no opinion"; one stage says "we are the critic." This is BollyAI's literal brand ("Har Friday ka faisla") rendered as a grid that contradicts it.
- **The diary loop as the product** — *Letterboxd*: users log a film *the second the credits roll*, racing to beat last year's count. Mechanism: the logged history is a sunk-cost identity asset; leaving means abandoning your own museum. Year-in-Review (gated at ≥10 films, drops Jan 8) is the dopamine payoff that compounds the loop. **The diary, not the reviews, is why DAU is sticky** — and it needs no studio content, only the user's own taps.
- **Rating as a colored heatmap, not a number** — *Letterboxd*'s green rating-distribution bars + orange "liked" hearts on near-black. Mechanism: a *shape* (the bell curve of a film's ratings, the warmth of a profile's greens) reads pre-cognitively faster than digits, and the two-accent system (cool green = rated, warm orange = loved) gives the dark UI a living, data-driven glow instead of dead chrome. BollyMeter is currently a bar; it should be a *signature mark*.
- **Editorial typography as brand, not decoration** — *MUBI* (LL Riforma everywhere, custom Spin display) + *A24* (oversized grotesk, brutalist whitespace) + *Criterion* (essay-grade serif). Mechanism: when the *type system itself* is the logo, every page is unmistakably yours without a watermark. BollyAI has Fraunces — a genuinely taste-tier variable serif — but uses it at content-card scale, wasting its theatricality.
- **Poster discipline + graceful posterless states** — *Letterboxd*'s rigid 2:3 grid with consistent gutters; *MUBI*'s full-bleed single key-art. Mechanism: identical aspect ratio + identical corner radius + identical shadow = the grid reads as *one curated object*, so even mediocre stills feel intentional. A *missing* poster is never an apology box — Letterboxd renders a clean typographic tile that looks deliberate.
- **Art-direction-as-scarcity-event** — *A24*'s merch drops + *MUBI*'s Notebook print magazine + Year-in-Review microsites. Mechanism: a recurring *designed moment* (not just content) trains the audience to return on a calendar. The ritual is the retention.
- **Dark UI with warmth, never cold** — *Criterion/MUBI* push near-black toward warm charcoal, not blue-black; accents are amber/green/red (cinema gels), never SaaS-blue. Mechanism: warmth signals "cinema/projection," coldness signals "dashboard." BollyAI's bg is `oklch(11% .03 280)` — indigo-violet, slightly *cool*; the taste tier would pull hue toward warm-neutral.

## WHERE BOLLYAI FAILS TODAY  (grounded in the screenshot + live site + tokens)
- **The hero is a democracy.** In the screenshot, the "Karuppu" lead tile and the "House of the Dragon" tile sit at near-identical visual weight, side by side — the eye ping-pongs and lands nowhere. There is no stage, no single sacred verdict. This directly betrays "Har Friday ka faisla."
- **Monogram placeholders read as broken, not curated.** Several tiles show initials boxes (per the brief, the live coverage gap). On a *cinema* site this caps perceived quality hard — and they look like *errors*, not Letterboxd-style intentional typographic tiles.
- **Zero retention loop.** No watchlist, no "seen it," no diary, no recap, no reason for the same visitor to return Friday-after-Friday. The brand's entire premise is recurring verdicts, but the site captures *nothing* of the user's relationship to them. This is the single biggest psychological gap vs the lens.
- **BollyMeter is a generic bar.** The verdict score — the brand's signature — renders as a forgettable compact meter inside cards, indistinguishable from any review-site rating. No heatmap, no signature, no scarcity around it.
- **Fraunces is under-deployed.** A theatrical variable serif is reduced to card-title duty. The taste tier would let it carry a 8rem+ billboard verdict line.
- **Cool palette + grain on weak images = muddy.** Indigo-violet bg (hue 280) is faintly cold, and grain/scanline overlays on top of already-low-res harvested stills compound into murk rather than mood.
- **Nav crams 8 desks + search into a thin strip** (visible top of screenshot) — reads busy/utilitarian, the opposite of MUBI's restraint.

## TOP 5 CONCRETE CHANGES  (ranked; each implementable)
1. **Replace FeaturedMosaic's bento with a single full-bleed "Aaj ka Faisla" billboard stage.** 78vh, full-bleed backdrop (official still) with a warm-black gradient scrim `linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 0%, oklch(8% .02 60 / .55) 45%, oklch(8% .02 60 / .96) 100%)`. Overlay: desk-accent verdict chip top-left; the film title in Fraunces at `clamp(3.5rem, 8.5vw, 8rem)`, weight 340, optical-size max; one-line verdict sentence in Hanken below; the **BollyMeter as a single large signature ring** (see #3) bottom-right; ONE primary CTA "Poora faisla padho →". The 8 secondary bento tiles demote into a quieter **"Is hafte aur" (also this week)** 5-up rail *below* the fold — they stop competing with the verdict. This makes the homepage *say its own tagline*.
2. **Ship a localStorage diary: "Meri Diary" (faux-Letterboxd, no login).** Three taps on every MediaCard + verdict page: **"Watchlist +"**, **"Dekhi ✓" (seen)**, **"❤ Pasand" (liked)** — all persisted to `localStorage.bollyai_diary`. A `/meri-diary` static page renders the user's own grid: counts ("12 dekhi · 8 watchlist · 5 pasand"), a desk-split donut, a Fraunces "Aapka 2026" header. This is pure client JS, ₹0, no backend — and it manufactures the exact sunk-cost return loop the brand lacks. Add a small **"Year in Film" generator** (build-time template + client data) that drops every Jan as the calendar ritual.
3. **Promote BollyMeter into the signature mark: a conic verdict ring + a rating heatmap.** (a) Per-title hero: a `conic-gradient` ring filling 0→score×36deg, desk-accent stroke on warm-black, score in JetBrains at center, "basis" caption beneath — this becomes the recognizable BollyAI glyph (the way Letterboxd's green bars are *theirs*). (b) On desk/yearboard pages, render a **verdict-distribution heatmap**: a 0–10 row of cells warming from `text-faint`→accent by how many titles scored there. Editorial-only data, honesty-fence-safe (no AggregateRating).
4. **Kill monogram placeholders; ship "designed posterless tiles."** When no `poster.jpg`, render a deliberate typographic poster: 2:3 tile, desk-accent flat wash at ~14% chroma, the title set *large* in Fraunces rotated/stacked, a small "press still pending" footnote, identical radius+shadow to real posters. It must read as a *choice* (Criterion-grade), never an error box. One shared `<PosterlessTile>` component; the grid instantly looks curated even at partial coverage.
5. **Warm the palette + restrain the nav.** Shift bg hue 280→~60 toward warm-charcoal (`--bg: oklch(11% .02 60)`, `--bg-deep: oklch(8% .018 60)`) so the whole site reads "projection booth," not "dashboard"; gate grain/scanline to `≥1px` backdrops only (skip on low-res stills) and drop grain opacity ~30%. Collapse the 8-desk nav into a single **"Desks ▾"** disclosure + a prominent ⌘K-style search pill (the only two things in a tall, airy bar) — MUBI restraint over the current cram.

## LATEST TECH / UI NOTE  (2026 primitives inside the static-export + ₹0 fence)
- **Cross-document View Transitions API** (`@view-transition { navigation: auto }` + `view-transition-name` on the hero poster) — gives MUBI-grade poster-morph navigation card→verdict page with **zero JS and zero framework**; ships in a static export, degrades silently in old browsers. This is the single highest-leverage "latest UI" win and it costs ~15 lines of CSS.
- **CSS `@property` + `conic-gradient`** for the animated BollyMeter ring fill (register `--meter` as `<angle>`, animate on scroll-into-view) — GPU-cheap, no animation library, honors the "no framer-motion" fence.
- **`scroll-driven animations`** (`animation-timeline: view()`) for the secondary rails to fade/rise on enter — native, zero-JS motion language to answer the "feels static" diagnosis.
- **`color-mix()` in OKLCH** to derive every per-desk accent tint from one base token at build/runtime — collapses the 7 hand-tuned desk accents into one system, keeping the hand-written-CSS ethos without a kit.
- All four are pure CSS/standard-DOM: no Tailwind, no shadcn, no server. They *extend* the existing globals.css rather than replacing the craft.

## ANTI-SLOP WARNING  (the generic redesign trap for this lens)
- **Do not literally clone Letterboxd green-on-black.** That is *their* glyph; cloning it makes BollyAI a knockoff, not a peer. Keep Fraunces + the warm amber/per-desk accents — BollyAI's distinctiveness is the *Indian-cinema warmth* and the disclosed-AI-critic voice, not the colorway. Steal the *mechanisms* (scarcity, diary, heatmap), not the skin.
- **Don't gamify into a fake-social-proof lie.** The diary must stay *private/local* and the brand's honesty fence forbids AggregateRating/vote-counts. Never surface "1.2k users rated" — there are no users server-side. Letterboxd's counts are real; ours would be fabricated. The diary is a *personal mirror*, not a leaderboard.
- **Don't let "editorial = sparse" become empty.** MUBI scarcity works because the *one* thing is gorgeous. With incomplete poster coverage, a single full-bleed hero is only as good as its still — so the hero must *only* feature titles that have real key-art (build-time gate), or the posterless treatment must be genuinely beautiful. A scarce hero with a monogram box is worse than the bento.
- **Don't drown the new hero in grain/vignette/scanline at once.** The current overlays already muddy weak images; piling MUBI-mood filters on top is the slop trap. Mood comes from *type + scrim + restraint*, not from stacking texture passes.

## ONE BIG SWING  (the "kamaal" move only this lens sees)
**"BollyAI Wrapped" — a no-login, build-time + localStorage "Aapka Saal in Film" that anyone can generate and screenshot-share, turning the visitor's own taps into BollyAI's distribution engine.** Letterboxd's Year-in-Review is its single biggest organic-virality event; BollyAI can have one *without accounts* because the diary lives in localStorage. A static `/wrapped` route renders, from the user's own logged data, a vertical share-card sequence (Fraunces headline "Aapne 2026 mein 23 filmein dekhi", desk-split donut, "Aapki sabse zyada dekhi gayi industry: Tollywood", their top BollyMeter agreements/disagreements vs BollyAI's verdicts — *"You loved 3 films BollyAI panned"* as a delicious tension hook), each frame export-as-PNG via a tiny canvas snapshot, watermarked `bollyai.in`. It is ₹0, server-free, on-brand (the AI-critic vs the human-viewer tension is the *whole identity*), and it makes the audience *market the site to their friends every January* — the one retention-and-acquisition flywheel the entire current homepage completely lacks.
