# Lens 03 — The Streaming-Platform UI Language (Netflix / Prime / JioHotstar / Disney+ / Apple TV+)

## VERDICT (3 sentences max)
Every Indian viewer's eye is pre-trained on ONE grammar for "a movie site": a full-bleed **billboard hero** on top, then **lazy horizontal rails** of hover-reactive cards below — BollyAI's equal-weight bento wall reads as a directory, not a cinema stage, so the eye has nothing to land on and nothing to chase. Adopt the billboard + rail skeleton wholesale (it is muscle-memory, not imitation), but render it in **Apple TV+'s restrained editorial register** (big type, generous air, no autoplay-video clutter) rather than Netflix's dense maximalism — that premium-critic posture is exactly BollyAI's "Har Friday ka faisla" brand. Crucially, all of this is now achievable **CSS-only, zero-JS, zero-library** (2026 scroll-snap carousels + `transform`-hover), so it fits the static-export + ₹0 fence with room to spare.

## WHAT THE BEST DO  (named examples + the MECHANISM — why it works)
- **Billboard hero = single high-conviction stage** — *Netflix, JioHotstar, Disney+ Hotstar home* — one full-bleed backdrop, oversized title art, one-line logline, ONE primary CTA, dark gradient scrim. Mechanism: it *solves "what do I look at"* in <1s by removing choice; a commanding focal point is what makes a page feel directed rather than dumped. **Netflix's own June-2026 web refresh is moving toward a more prominent hero banner + TV-style rows** — so a bento hero is behind even Netflix's current direction.
- **Gradient scrim as the depth+legibility trick** — *every platform* — a `linear-gradient` from transparent→~0.9 opaque over the bottom-left lets bold white type sit on any still without a box. Mechanism: it manufactures cinematic depth and guarantees contrast *for free*, no design work per-image — exactly what BollyAI needs when poster quality is uneven.
- **Lazy horizontal rails ("rows")** — *Netflix, Prime, Hotstar* — content lives in named, horizontally-scrolling rails ("Big This Week", "OTT This Week"), not a static grid. Mechanism: a rail signals *abundance beyond the fold* and invites a low-commitment side-swipe; the partially-clipped card at the right edge is a deliberate "there's more" affordance that pulls horizontal exploration.
- **"Top 10" ranked rail with giant numerals** — *Netflix's signature row* — huge outlined `1…10` digits behind each poster. Mechanism: social proof + scarcity + a finishable list ("only 10") — the single most-clicked Netflix row. BollyAI owns a *real, defensible* ranking signal (BollyMeter / box-office order) that Netflix fakes with engagement — this is a gift.
- **Card hover = scale + metadata reveal** — *Netflix, Prime* — on hover the card scales (~1.1–1.25), lifts, and an overlay fades in title+meta+CTA (`opacity 0→1`). Mechanism: rewards intent with information *in place* (no navigation), and the micro-motion makes a static page feel alive and responsive — the "latest UI" feel the brief says is missing.
- **Progress / continue bars** — *Hotstar, Prime* — a thin fill bar under a card. Mechanism: it implies a living, in-use system. BollyAI has no accounts, but the *visual language* maps to a **BollyMeter fill bar** or a box-office-run progress bar — same trained read ("this is tracked"), zero login.
- **Apple TV+'s premium restraint** — *tv.apple.com* — fewer, larger, editorially-curated tiles; massive headline type; generous vertical rhythm; near-black canvas; almost no chrome. Mechanism: *spacing as structure* + a confident type scale reads as authority and taste. This is the posture of a critic, not a catalog — the correct register for a disclosed-AI verdict brand.

## WHERE BOLLYAI FAILS TODAY  (grounded in the screenshot + code + live site)
- **The hero is a bento, not a stage.** In the screenshot the "Karuppu" lead tile (Rs 304.1–308.8 cr) sits at *roughly the same visual weight* as Game-of-Thrones/Dune, Dhokha, Pebble, Mollywood Times — the eye bounces across ~9 competing tiles and lands nowhere. There is no billboard, no single focal point, no "look here first."
- **No depth, no scrim, no motion.** Tiles are flat rectangles on the dark indigo bg; nothing scales, lifts, or reveals on hover; there is no gradient overlay turning a still into a cinematic plate. The page reads "static directory," precisely the "feels static vs latest UI" diagnosis.
- **Monogram placeholders mid-grid are brutal at hero scale.** On a *cinema* site, a posterless title rendering initials inside the headline mosaic caps perceived quality instantly. A billboard built around ONE guaranteed-great press still sidesteps this; the bento *forces* placeholders into the most prominent real estate.
- **Rails exist but don't behave like rails.** "Just Dropped" / "Big This Week" are horizontal media-rails, but with no hover-scale, no metadata reveal, no ranked numerals, no edge-peek tension — they read as static strips, not the live, swipeable rows viewers expect.
- **Nav crams 7 desks + search into a thin strip** (visible top bar: Bollywood/Kollywood/Tollywood/Mollywood/Sandalwood/Hollywood/Streaming + search + "Box Office Now" + "OTT Calendar") — busy and un-premium; the opposite of Apple TV+'s calm chrome.
- **Grain + scanline overlays on weak/low-res stills** muddy already-soft images, dragging perceived quality *down* rather than adding texture — texture should live on the scrim/UI chrome, not on top of every poster.

## TOP 5 CONCRETE CHANGES  (ranked; each implementable)
1. **Kill the bento. Build a full-bleed billboard hero (`<HeroBillboard>`).** Replace `FeaturedMosaic`'s lead-tile-in-grid with: a **72vh** full-bleed backdrop of the single biggest verdict of the week (best-available press still — never a monogram); Fraunces title at `clamp(3.5rem, 8vw, 7.5rem)`; a one-line logline in Hanken Grotesk `--text-dim`; a **BollyMeter verdict chip top-left** (`X.X/10` in JetBrains Mono, desk-accent ring); ONE primary CTA ("Padho faisla →"); a gradient scrim `linear-gradient(105deg, oklch(8% .025 280 / .92) 0%, /.55 38%, transparent 70%)` anchoring the bottom-left text block. Selection is **build-time** (top BollyMeter × box-office of the week) — no server needed.
2. **Demote the 8 bento tiles into the first real rail.** Everything that was fighting the hero becomes rail #1 ("Is hafte ka faisla") *below* the billboard — same cards, now in a horizontal scroll-snap row, so the hero owns the fold alone and abundance lives in the swipe.
3. **Ship CSS-only hover on `MediaCard`:** `transition: transform .28s ease, box-shadow .28s; transform-origin` set per-position (`left` for first-in-row, `right` for last, else center) so scale doesn't clip at rail edges; `:hover/:focus-within → transform: scale(1.12) translateY(-6px)` + lift `--shadow-poster`; an absolutely-positioned `.card__reveal` overlay (desk label · title · meta · BollyMeter bar) transitions `opacity 0→1`. **Zero JS, zero library** — pure CSS satisfies the static-export fence. Gate the whole thing behind `@media (hover:hover)` and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. **Build a real "Top 10" rail using BollyMeter / box-office order (`<RankedRail>`).** Giant outlined numerals (Fraunces or JetBrains Mono, `font-size: clamp(5rem,12vw,11rem)`, `-webkit-text-stroke: 2px --border`, fill transparent) bleeding behind each poster. This is **legitimately ranked** (real box-office / verdict order, computed at build) — BollyAI does honestly what Netflix fakes with engagement, and it directly showcases BollyMeter. Add a `--rank-label` ("by box office" / "by BollyMeter") so the basis is always disclosed, per the no-fake-numbers fence.
5. **Re-register the whole page in the Apple TV+ key:** widen section vertical rhythm (≥`clamp(4rem,8vw,7rem)` between rails), let the hero + each rail-heading breathe, thin the global chrome (collapse the 7-desk nav into a single "Desks ▾" menu or a quieter second row so the top bar is calm), and **strip film-grain/scanline off poster imagery** — keep texture only on the scrim and section backgrounds. Restraint is the brand: a critic's page, not a catalog wall.

## LATEST TECH / UI NOTE  (2026 primitive within the static-export + ₹0 fence)
- **Native CSS carousels (CSS Overflow L5)** — `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory` + `::scroll-button()` + `::scroll-marker()` (Chrome 135+ / Safari 18.2+, Mar/Dec 2025) generate accessible prev/next + dot markers **straight from the scroll container with no button markup and no JS**. This is the rail mechanism BollyAI should adopt: premium, accessible, 60fps on the compositor thread, and it ships **0 bytes of JavaScript** — perfect for static export. Provide a graceful baseline (plain `overflow-x:auto` scroll-snap) for Firefox, which still lags on the new pseudo-elements as of mid-2026.
- **Scroll-driven animations** (`animation-timeline: view()`) — fade/parallax the billboard scrim and rank numerals on scroll, compositor-threaded, again **no JS, no framer-motion**. Stay inside the existing plain-CSS + OKLCH system; no Tailwind/library needed, which keeps the anti-slop + ₹0 fences intact.
- **`@view-transition { navigation: auto }`** — one CSS block gives cross-page poster→detail morphs (the "shared element" feel Netflix/Hotstar get from SPA routing) on a *static multi-page* site. Directly answers the brief's "no view-transitions, feels static."

## ANTI-SLOP WARNING  (the generic redesign trap for this lens)
- **Do NOT clone Netflix red-on-black maximalism.** A dense wall of identical rounded thumbnails + a red CTA is the single most generic "streaming clone" tell — it would bury BollyAI's bespoke Fraunces/OKLCH craft under cookie-cutter SaaS. Keep the *skeleton* (billboard + rails + hover), import the *restraint* from Apple TV+, and render it in BollyAI's own warm-amber/desk-accent palette and serif voice.
- **Do NOT fake "Continue Watching", "Because you watched", "Trending Now (live)" or any progress that implies a logged-in/personalized session** — BollyAI has no accounts and no view data; faking it violates the honesty-as-brand fence. Map the *visual* (progress bar, ranked row) onto *honest* signals only: BollyMeter fill, real box-office run, build-time verdict order — each shown with its basis.
- **Do NOT chase autoplay-video hero previews.** No backend, no video pipeline, and it would wreck LCP on a static site. A still + scrim + CSS Ken-Burns drift is the static-friendly equivalent and reads as premium, not cheap.
- **Do NOT let placeholders into the billboard.** Better to rotate the build-time hero to a title that *has* a real press still than to hero a monogram. Coverage gaps stay in the rails (smaller blast radius), never on the stage.

## ONE BIG SWING  (the "kamaal" move only this lens sees)
**The "Friday Marquee" — a build-time, scroll-snap billboard that is BollyAI's signature, not Netflix's.** A full-bleed hero that **paginates as a 3–5 frame scroll-snap deck** (`::scroll-marker()` dots), each frame the week's biggest *verdict* (not just biggest release): the still under a cinematic scrim, the **BollyMeter score punched in giant Fraunces with a verdict-ladder rung** ("Blockbuster faisla" / "Skip kar do"), the ≥2-source box-office figure in JetBrains Mono with its citation pill, and one CTA. Because BollyAI's differentiator is the *verdict* — "Har Friday ka faisla" — the hero literally becomes that headline, the way a newspaper front page leads with its lead story, not a poster catalog. It fuses the streaming billboard (familiar muscle-memory) with the *critic's front page* (BollyAI's actual brand) — Netflix can't build this because Netflix has no verdict to lead with. CSS-only, zero-JS, ships inside the ₹0 static fence, and turns the homepage's weakest spot into the loudest expression of what BollyAI uniquely is.
